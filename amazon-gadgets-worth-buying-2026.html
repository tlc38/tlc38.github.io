2 · BEST CAR UPGRADE

Portable Wireless CarPlay / Android Auto Screen

If your car has a usable stereo but no modern phone interface, a portable dashboard screen can add wireless CarPlay or Android Auto without replacing the factory head unit. Look for a bright screen, stable mounting, straightforward audio routing and a backup-camera option only if you actually need it.

Best for: older vehicles you plan to keep. Check before buying: dash space, power-cable routing and how audio will reach your speakers.

These slim magnetic light bars are useful under kitchen cabinets, in closets, pantries and dark hallways. Rechargeable models avoid disposable batteries, while motion mode means you do not need to reach for a switch.

Best for: adding light without wiring. Check before buying: USB-C charging, adjustable brightness, color-temperature choices and removable magnetic mounting.

4 · MOST FUN

Portable 1080p Mini Projector

A small projector can turn a bedroom, basement or backyard wall into a big-screen movie setup for far less than a giant TV. Native 1080p is a sensible baseline; built-in streaming can be convenient, but a separate streaming stick is often easier to replace later.

Best for: occasional movie nights and portable big-screen viewing. Check before buying: native resolution, real brightness information, throw distance, focus/keystone controls and app support.
