Best HDMI cables for PS5

The cable included with a PS5 is already suitable for 4K at 120Hz. Buy another cable only when you need a longer run, a replacement, a second connection—or premium construction for a demanding installation.

What matters for PS5

  • 4K at 120Hz: Your television must support 4K/120Hz on the specific HDMI input being used, and the game must offer a compatible mode.
  • Ultra High Speed certification: This is more meaningful than vague phrases such as “8K cable” or “gaming cable.”
  • Length: Buy only as long as necessary. For genuinely long routes, an active optical cable is often the safer category.
  • VRR and HDR: A compliant 48Gbps cable can carry the features; the console, game and display must support and enable them.
  • No visual upgrade from excess bandwidth: A premium or 96Gbps cable does not add detail, color or frame rate when a functioning 48Gbps cable already carries the full signal.

Our recommendation

For a typical setup, start with the Zeskit Maya or a Cable Matters certified 48Gbps cable in the shortest comfortable length. Choose Monoprice Slim when routing space is tight, and move to RUIPRO or another reputable active optical design only for a long run. The premium options are legitimate choices for construction, installation requirements or future planning—but they are not required for PS5 performance.
